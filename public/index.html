Integrity matters

Our promise to donors and applicants

Verification before publication

Identity and supporting documents must be reviewed securely by authorized staff before a case is approved. Public pages should disclose only information the applicant has agreed to share.

Clear financial records

Pledges, received funds, fees, disbursements, and refunds must be recorded separately and reconciled against provider statements.

Responsible assistance

Where possible, assistance should be paid directly to verified creditors or service providers with beneficiary consent and proper receipts.
